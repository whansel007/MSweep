How to Play

You are a Butterfly Watch volunteer surveying patches of Singapore habitat.

Reveal cells to log butterflies. Number cells show how many butterflies are in the 8 neighbours.

Start at the sunny edges — hints cluster there (real surveys found 85 species at edges vs 63 in shade). The dark interior is sparse.

Cryptic butterflies look almost like empty cells. Use the Flag Cryptic tool to keep them alive. Use Mark Extinct only if you believe they're gone — wrong extinctions lower your score.

Rediscovery: A cell marked extinct may later be found to be a surviving cryptic species — bonus points!

Keyboard: Arrow keys to move cursor, Space/Enter to use active tool, F to flag cryptic, X to mark extinct, R to reveal.

Real Singapore totals: 334 extant species, 153 extirpated. Try to build the fullest record!
